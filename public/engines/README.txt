Engines used by the "play my bot" chess game on this site.

stockfish-19-lite-single.js / .wasm
  Stockfish 19, single-threaded lite WebAssembly build, by the Stockfish
  developers, compiled by Nathan Rugg (stockfish.js, npm "stockfish" 19.0.1).
  Licence: GNU GPL v3, see STOCKFISH-COPYING.txt.
  Source: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
          https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js

maia-1500.onnx
  Maia (McIlroy-Young, Sen, Kleinberg, Anderson: "Aligning Superhuman AI with
  Human Behavior: Chess as a Model System", KDD 2020), the 1500-rated network,
  converted to ONNX by the lczerolens project.
  Source: https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-chess (weights and code: GPL v3)
          https://huggingface.co/spaces/lczerolens/backends-demo
